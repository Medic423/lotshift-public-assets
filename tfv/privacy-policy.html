Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

LotShift ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Ferrell Creative LLC, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our vehicle move coordination platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Dealership organization details and driver roster information
  • Phone numbers for SMS notifications
  • Location data for service coordination

2.2 Usage Information

We automatically collect information about how you use our platform, including move requests, driver offers, and system interactions.

3. SMS Notifications

When you opt-in to receive SMS notifications, we will send you text messages regarding:

  • Transactional vehicle move offers (VIN/stock #, origin and destination rooftops, ready time) with optional reply YES or NO
  • Move award and dispatch updates related to offers you received
  • Important system notifications

Opt-in: Drivers receive SMS only after a dealership administrator adds the driver's mobile number on the People → Drivers screen and confirms that the driver may receive transactional LotShift dispatch texts (offers and awards). Drivers may also opt in by providing their mobile number to their employer for entry into LotShift.

Opt-Out: You can opt-out of SMS notifications at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any LotShift SMS, or having a dealership administrator remove the mobile number from the driver profile in People → Drivers
  • Replying "STOP" to any SMS message
  • Contacting us at chuck41090@mac.com

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on move and offer activity.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Facilitate vehicle move coordination between dealership dispatchers and their drivers
  • Send transactional SMS to drivers who have been opted in (see Section 3)
  • Improve our services and platform functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

5. Information Sharing

We share your information only as necessary to provide our services:

  • With drivers you assign when moves are dispatched
  • With your dealership when you accept or decline move offers
  • With service providers who assist in platform operations
  • When required by law or to protect rights and safety

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Opt-out of SMS notifications at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to certain processing of your information
